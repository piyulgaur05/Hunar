# Hunaré visual system

Brand: **Hunaré — Where Every Craft Tells a Story.** "Hunar" means skill, the kind that lives in a pair of hands. The accented "é" is the signature flourish: always set in italic terracotta with Fraunces' SOFT and WONK axes turned up.

Storefront: a storybook of craft. Warm paper with a faint grain, ink typography, terracotta accents, marigold highlights and an indigo "night" chapter for the makers. Fraunces (variable, optical size, SOFT/WONK axes) carries the voice; Manrope carries the interface. The page is organised as numbered chapters, and a stitched thread pinned to the left edge is sewn as the reader scrolls. Imagery uses arch-topped (jharokha) and single-corner-rounded crops. Controls are pill-shaped with a dashed inner "stitch". Product cards reveal the story of the piece (maker, origin, material) on hover. The newsletter is a tilted postcard with a perforated edge and a turning seal for a stamp. The footer closes with an outlined, oversized wordmark. Motion is slow, scroll-driven and respects reduced-motion.

Admin: warm white work surfaces, dark fixed navigation, compact readable tables, explicit status badges, focused editors and useful empty states. Charts communicate real persisted data.

Tokens live in packages/ui/src/tokens.css. Interactive targets are at least 44px; keyboard focus is explicit; native inputs and semantic landmarks are preferred. Dialogs use accessible primitives. Layouts adapt at 640px and 1024px. Motion respects reduced-motion preferences.
